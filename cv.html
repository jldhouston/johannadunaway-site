Areas of Expertise

  • Substantive: Political communication; news media and politics; local news; partisan media and polarization; mobile and digital news consumption; trust in media and misinformation; political knowledge, attitudes, and behavior.
  • Methods: Survey design and question wording; survey and laboratory experiments; cross-platform (mobile versus desktop) experimental design; large-scale content analysis; eye tracking; psychophysiological measurement; local news data and mapping.
